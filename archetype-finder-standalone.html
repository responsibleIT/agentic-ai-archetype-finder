    How the result is determined

    0 · The scales

    Nine dimensions have three anchors and take the scores 1, 3 or 5 only. Control flow, tool use and human role have five named levels and take 1 to 5. Every score is a named position; there are no in-between values to interpret.

    1 · The gate: is it agentic?

    Take the five agency dimensions: control flow, tool use, underspecification, goal directedness, long term planning. The agency level is the highest of the five scores, never their sum. 1 is none (not agentic), 2 low, 3 moderate, 4 and 5 high. A system is agentic when any of the five is 2 or more, because that is where a model first decides something at run time. The other seven dimensions do not count toward the gate: memory or learning make a system adaptive, not agentic; multiplicity and human role say what kind of agentic system it is.

    2 · The match: which shape is it closest to?

    The gate splits the six archetypes into two groups: Not agentic stands alone; the other five are agentic. A system is compared only with the archetypes in its own group, so a non-agentic profile always lands on Not agentic and the match then only says how far it is from the pure case. Distance is the sum, over the twelve dimensions, of the absolute difference between the system's score and the archetype's score; the nearest archetype is the one with the smallest distance, ties going to the earlier one in the list. Match % is 1 − distance / 48, where 48 is the largest possible distance (12 × 4). Scores are ordinal, so distance ranks archetypes and nothing more.

    3 · The notes

    Rules fire on combinations of the profile and the three context attributes. They never change the match; context enters only here.

    One source per dimension: Schonenberg et al. 2008, with van der Aalst (control flow), Mialon et al. 2023 (tool use), Chan et al. 2023 (underspecification, directness of impact, goal directedness, long term planning), Sumers et al. 2023 (memory), Russell & Norvig 2021 (learning), Wooldridge 2009 (number of agents, orchestration), Hammond et al. 2025 (ownership), Feng, McDonald & Zhang 2025 (human role; corroborated by Morris et al. 2024). Context attributes, reversibility, error tolerance, data sensitivity, after IMDA 2026; they do not enter the match.
    Whether a system is agentic is a gate, not a match: the highest score on control flow, tool use, underspecification, goal directedness and long term planning, agentic from 2 up. The archetype match then runs within that group. Distance is the sum of absolute differences across the twelve dimensions. The form of the input and output (structured or free text) is deliberately not a dimension: it describes the interface, not the agency, and it was pulling the match around.
